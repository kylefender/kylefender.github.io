Hey everyone!

Welcome to my brand new corner of the internet! If you're reading this, it means I've successfully navigated the (sometimes tricky, but ultimately rewarding) waters of setting up a blog.

My weapon of choice for this particular endeavor was the beautiful and feature-rich [jekyll-theme-chirpy](https://github.com/cotes2020/jekyll-theme-chirpy). Seriously, if you're looking for a clean, modern, and highly customizable theme, Chirpy is an absolute gem. It comes with so many great features right out of the box, from dark mode to search functionality, making it a joy to work with.

One tool that made this whole process significantly smoother was [jekyll-compose](https://github.com/jekyll/jekyll-compose). For those unfamiliar, jekyll-compose is a plugin that provides helpful commands for creating new posts, drafts, and pages directly from your terminal. No more manually creating new .md files and front matter – just a quick command and you're good to go. It's a small thing, but it saves so much time and keeps your workflow streamlined.
